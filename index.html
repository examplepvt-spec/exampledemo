Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Our cuts are designed with modern architectural European tailoring. We recommend consulting our detailed dimensional sizing calculator on each product page or booking a quick virtual consultation with our stylists.

We offer hassle-free 30-day returns and exchanges worldwide. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags attached. Return shipping labels are fully prepaid.

We source 100% GOTS-certified organic cotton, RMS-certified ethical wool, and OEKO-TEX certified dyes. Every partner atelier undergoes annual third-party social and environmental audits.

We recommend hand washing in cold water with organic wool/silk detergent or professional eco-dry cleaning. Each garment comes with a specialized cedar storage pouch to protect against elements.
